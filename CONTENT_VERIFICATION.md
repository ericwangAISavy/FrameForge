## Status vocabulary

Verified: owner supplied supporting evidence and approved public use.
Unverified: factual claim awaiting evidence; excluded from public factual claims.
Illustrative: educational analysis or draft editorial text, not personal production history.
Independent concept: concept exploration, without a claim of a completed game.
Demonstrated implementation: browser functionality actually present in this repository.
Planned or experimental: proposed work, not completed functionality.
